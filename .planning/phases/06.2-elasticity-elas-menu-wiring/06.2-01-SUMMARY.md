### Auto-fixed issues

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug] Pipe escaping in FR/EN label cells broke validator**

- **Found during:** Task 1 (after initial `Write` of the audit)
- **Issue:** Plan Task 1 Step 11 specificity note said to "Escape pipes `|` in description strings as `\|` for GitHub-Markdown safety." Actual result: `tools/validate_audit_md.py` uses a primitive `line.strip("|").split("|")` parser that does NOT honour backslash escaping, so `DRAWING of DISPLACEMENT \| STRESS` split into 11 (correct) + 1 (spillover) = 12 tokens, triggering `rule 0 (9 cols per row) failed at row 7`.
- **Fix:** Replaced all 8 occurrences of ` \| ` with ` & ` (ampersand for "and/or" — matches FR-label convention already present in source files like `td/ma/contdepl`). Ran `sed -i 's/ \\| / \& /g'` over the audit markdown. FR/EN content semantics preserved.
- **Files modified:** `.planning/phases/06.2-elasticity-elas-menu-wiring/LEXICON-AUDIT-elas.md`
